This is the first post on a blog that should have existed some time ago.
I have been putting it off for too long, and it is time to take the plaster off.

## What will be here

Three things, in order of likelihood.

**Working notes from public health.** Indicators, queries, prescribing-appropriateness
criteria, data quality and audit. Things I end up building or rebuilding every week,
and for which I almost never find a good, concise explanation anywhere else.

**Product reflections on [MediFlow](https://getmediflow.dev/en).** Architectural decisions,
trade-offs and why I chose one thing instead of another. No marketing. The most useful
thing I can do with a blog is explain honestly why a choice makes sense, even when
someone could quite reasonably choose differently.

**More scattered pieces.** Things I read, tools I use and the occasional technical
digression. Nothing compulsory, no fixed schedule.

## What will not be here

No tracking cookies, no analytics, no resources loaded from third parties, no newsletter
with a double opt-in that then ends up in spam. If you want to follow along, there is
the [RSS feed](/en/rss.xml). It still works perfectly well in 2026.

## A note on style

I try to keep each post under a thousand words. When I need to be longer, I split it
into parts. No baroque formatting, no grocery-list bullet points, no emoji at the start
of a paragraph. Dry English, a little technical language where it helps, always
explained the first time.

Enjoy the reading.
